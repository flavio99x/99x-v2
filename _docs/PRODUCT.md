## Platform

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP 3.13 (ScrollTrigger, SplitText) + Lenis, three.js via React Three Fiber (3D logo only). Content as typed TS files (CMS-ready). Deployed on Vercel. Chosen by the user.

## Product

99x is a Norwegian-rooted technology company (founded 2000) with ~600 people across Norway, Sri Lanka, Portugal, Brazil and Malaysia (confirm list). It sells four lines of business:

- **Product Engineering** — full lifecycle engineering, AI, quality engineering & test automation, cloud consulting, product discovery, due diligence, Optimaxer AI Web.
- **Nearshore Tech Talent — Europe** — software development, QA & testing, cloud engineering, data science & AI.
- **Nearshore Tech Talent — USA/Americas** — AI-enabled nearshore engineers from Brazil for US companies: staff augmentation, web apps, mobile, AI & data.
- **Web Solutions** — e-commerce and web platforms.

Positioning: "Norwegian values, global capacity" — advisors, builders and innovators who build digital products, scale teams and integrate AI.

## Users

Decision-makers (CTOs, VPs Engineering, product leaders, founders) at companies in the Nordics, Europe and the US looking for an engineering partner or nearshore talent. Secondary: candidates (careers) and partners.

## Website job

Present 99x as a global brand and generate leads across all markets (contact form + division emails + named contacts).
